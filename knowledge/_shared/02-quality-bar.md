# Knowledge Quality Bar

## Purpose
Set minimum quality standards for sales guidance so reps can execute without guesswork.

## Scope
All files under `knowledge/sales/` and any future edits linked to GRO-379 architecture.

## Inputs
- Win/loss notes
- Recorded call snippets
- Objection trends
- Rep feedback on script usability

## Decision Rules
- Every tactical rule must be testable in a real call.
- Every major claim must map to an observable signal or evidence source.
- Every file must preserve contract section order.
- Prefer concise instructions over narrative filler.

## Examples
- Good rule: "End discovery with a dated next step, or mark as unqualified."
- Bad rule: "Keep the conversation positive" without operational instruction.

## Failure Modes
- Generic SaaS language without Grove-specific differentiation.
- Advice that cannot be scored with a rubric.
- Missing examples that show correct execution.
