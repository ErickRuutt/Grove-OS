# CS/Support Knowledge Pod

## Purpose
Standardize onboarding, support, and retention execution so Grove customers reach value quickly and stay on a high-trust, high-expansion path.

## Scope
Covers implementation kickoff, activation milestones, support triage and escalation, incident communications, renewal risk detection, and expansion signals.

## Inputs
- `_shared` standards: [taxonomy](../_shared/01-taxonomy.md), [quality bar](../_shared/02-quality-bar.md)
- Sales handoff notes and qualification context
- Product capability boundaries and known constraints
- Support ticket and onboarding trend data

## Decision Rules
- Prioritize time-to-first-operational-value over feature tours.
- Tie every onboarding and support action to business workflow outcomes.
- Escalate quickly when customer risk affects trust, timeline, or executive confidence.
- Treat renewal and expansion as outcome signals, not account-management scripts.

## Examples
- Use `playbooks/kickoff.md` to align owners, scope, and success criteria in week 1.
- Use `operations/incident-comms.md` to run clear customer communication during incidents.

## Failure Modes
- Onboarding plans with no owner accountability.
- Support responses that solve tickets but miss workflow root causes.
- Renewal conversations started too late to influence risk trajectory.
