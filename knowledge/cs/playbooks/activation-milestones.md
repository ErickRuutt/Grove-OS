# Activation Milestones Playbook

## Purpose
Drive early customer value by sequencing milestones that prove workflow reliability and team adoption.

## Scope
Days 1-60 of customer onboarding and adoption support.

## Inputs
- Kickoff plan and success metrics
- Customer workflow map
- Product usage telemetry and qualitative check-ins

## Decision Rules
- Define milestone criteria before configuration work starts.
- Track milestone health as `on_track`, `at_risk`, or `off_track` with evidence.
- Trigger intervention when two consecutive check-ins show no operational progress.
- Tie adoption asks to specific team workflows, not generic usage volume.

## Examples
- Milestone 1 (Day 14): first workflow live with owner accountability.
- Milestone 2 (Day 30): cross-functional handoff process run in Grove with repeatable quality.
- Milestone 3 (Day 45-60): executive review shows measurable process improvement.

## Failure Modes
- Measuring success with login counts alone.
- Milestones that reflect setup completion but not business execution change.
- Waiting for QBRs to surface activation risk.
