# Renewal Risk and Expansion Playbook

## Purpose
Detect renewal risk early and identify credible expansion opportunities based on delivered outcomes.

## Scope
Ongoing account health reviews from post-activation through renewal window.

## Inputs
- Activation milestone history
- Support trend and incident impact history
- Stakeholder engagement map and executive sponsor status
- Outcome metrics tied to original success criteria

## Decision Rules
- Start renewal risk review at least 120 days before contract end.
- Score risk as `low`, `medium`, or `high` with explicit evidence and owner.
- Require a mitigation plan for `medium` and `high` risk accounts within 7 days.
- Frame expansion only when current scope outcomes are proven and stable.

## Examples
- High risk: executive sponsor disengaged + missed milestones + repeated sev2 issues.
- Expansion signal: second team requests rollout after measurable value in first workflow.

## Failure Modes
- Treating renewal as a late-stage negotiation event.
- Pitching expansion while current implementation is unstable.
- Health scoring based on sentiment without operational evidence.
