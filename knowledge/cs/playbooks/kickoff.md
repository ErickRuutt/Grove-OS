# Implementation Kickoff Playbook

## Purpose
Launch new customer implementations with clear ownership, outcome targets, and a realistic 30-60 day execution path.

## Scope
First customer implementation meeting and immediate follow-up actions.

## Inputs
- Sales discovery and qualification notes
- Confirmed buyer goals and workflow pain points
- Current team/process map from customer stakeholders

## Decision Rules
- Confirm one accountable customer owner for rollout decisions.
- Convert goals into measurable 30-day activation milestones.
- Document current workflow failure modes before proposing Grove configuration.
- End kickoff with named owners, dated milestones, and weekly check-in cadence.

## Examples
- "Primary success metric: reduce cross-team handoff misses from weekly to near-zero by day 45."
- "Owner: Operations lead; Milestone 1 due in 10 days; risk review every Friday."

## Failure Modes
- Treating kickoff as product walkthrough instead of operating-plan alignment.
- Accepting vague success language with no measurable milestone.
- Leaving stakeholder ownership unresolved.
