# CS/Support QA Rubric

## Purpose
Score CS and Support execution artifacts for clarity, customer trust, and operational usefulness.

## Scope
Applies to kickoff plans, milestone trackers, support responses, incident updates, and renewal-risk summaries.

## Inputs
- Artifact content and timestamps
- Customer outcome and health signals
- [quality bar](../../_shared/02-quality-bar.md)

## Decision Rules
- Score each artifact 1-5 across: outcome clarity, owner accountability, risk handling, communication quality, and evidence strength.
- Pass threshold: average >= 4.0 and no category below 3.
- Any category below 3 requires a corrective action with owner and due date.

## Examples
- Score 5 communication quality: concise updates with impact, next action, and explicit timing.
- Score 2 evidence strength: status claims made without usage, milestone, or incident data.

## Failure Modes
- Inflated scores used to avoid hard follow-up.
- Health summaries with sentiment but no operational evidence.
- QA done without support and CS signal alignment.
