# CS/Support Golden Examples

## Purpose
Provide concrete examples of high-quality CS/Support execution outputs.

## Scope
Reference examples for kickoff, triage, incident communication, and renewal risk artifacts.

## Inputs
- [rubric](./rubric.md)
- Active customer account context
- Product and support evidence

## Decision Rules
- Each example must show explicit ownership, evidence, risk state, and next action.
- Keep examples realistic and operationally constrained.

## Examples
- Kickoff summary (strong):
  - Outcome: reduce missed handoffs by 50% in 45 days.
  - Owner: customer ops lead; Grove CSM partner named.
  - Milestones: day 14 workflow launch, day 30 cross-team adoption, day 45 KPI review.
  - Risks: data-owner bandwidth constraint; mitigation assigned with due date.

- Incident update (strong):
  - Impact: approval workflow delayed for 4 accounts.
  - Action: mitigation deployed; manual fallback shared.
  - Next update: 2:30 PM PT.
  - Confidence: root cause under investigation, no speculative claims.

## Failure Modes
- Examples that are generic templates with no business impact context.
- Outputs missing owner, date, or measurable outcome.
