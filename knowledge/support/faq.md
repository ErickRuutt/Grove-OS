## [ACCOUNT] — How do I reset my password?

**Q:** How do I reset my password?

**A:** To reset your password:
1. Go to the login page and click "Forgot password"
2. Enter the email address associated with your account
3. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for a reset link — it expires in 15 minutes
4. Click the link and enter a new password
5. Log in with your new password — clear browser cookies/cache if you still see an error

If you have reset your password and still cannot log in, escalate to Engineering/CTO — the account may be locked or there may be a credentials sync issue.

**Tags:** account, access, password-reset

## [ACCOUNT] — I reset my password but still can't log in

**Q:** I've reset my password (multiple times) but I keep getting "Invalid credentials" or am still locked out.

**A:** This is typically caused by one of the following:
1. **Account locked after too many failed attempts** — accounts lock after 5 consecutive failed logins. Wait 30 minutes and try again, or contact support for an immediate unlock.
2. **Browser cache/cookies** — your browser may be holding a stale session. Clear cookies for this site and try in an incognito/private window.
3. **Email mismatch** — confirm you are using the exact email address that was used to create the account (case-sensitive).
4. **Reset link expired** — password reset links expire after 15 minutes. If the link has expired, request a new one.
5. **Credentials sync delay** — after resetting, wait 60 seconds before attempting to log in.

If none of these resolve the issue, the account may require a manual reset by Engineering. Please provide your account email so we can escalate.

**Tags:** account, access, locked-out, invalid-credentials
