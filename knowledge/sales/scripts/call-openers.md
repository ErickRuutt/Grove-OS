# Call Openers

## Purpose
Provide concise opening scripts that frame Grove around business outcomes and process clarity.

## Scope
First 2-4 minutes of discovery and evaluation calls.

## Inputs
- Contact role
- Known process pain
- Meeting objective

## Decision Rules
- Start with why this conversation matters to their business process.
- Keep opener under 45 seconds, then transition to questions.
- Use language that differentiates Grove from generic chat tools.

## Examples
- "Thanks for joining. I want to focus this on where your team loses time with AI today, then see if Grove's business memory model is a fit."
- "We'll keep this practical: current process, failure cost, and whether there's a near-term path to improvement."

## Failure Modes
- Opening with feature tour framing.
- Overpromising before context is established.
- Sounding scripted to the point of losing trust.
