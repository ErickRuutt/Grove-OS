# Follow-up Emails

## Purpose
Standardize post-call follow-ups that lock next steps and preserve buying momentum.

## Scope
Post-discovery, post-evaluation, and post-proposal email follow-ups.

## Inputs
- Call summary notes
- Confirmed pain and owner
- Agreed next step and date

## Decision Rules
- Send within 4 business hours of meeting.
- Include: recap, decision-relevant insight, next action with owner/date.
- If no next step was agreed, request one directly and flag risk.

## Examples
- Subject: "Next step for Grove evaluation - June 3"
- Body: "You shared that onboarding handoffs are inconsistent across teams. Next step: 30-minute workflow mapping on June 3 with you and your CS lead."

## Failure Modes
- Generic thank-you notes without action.
- New claims not discussed in the call.
- Ambiguous or missing ownership of next step.
