# Golden Examples

## Purpose
Capture high-quality execution examples that reps can model directly.

## Scope
Sales call language and follow-up behavior aligned with Grove standards.

## Inputs
- Top-performing call excerpts
- Follow-up messages with strong conversion outcomes
- QA rubric references from [rubric](./rubric.md)

## Decision Rules
- Include only examples scoring >= 4 in all rubric categories.
- Keep examples anonymized and operational.
- Each example must include why it works and what to avoid.

## Examples
- Example 1: Discovery opener
  Why it works: Frames business pain first and positions Grove distinctly.
  Script: "Before we talk tools, I want to map where AI is breaking your operating rhythm and what that costs your team weekly."
  Avoid: "Let me show you our AI assistant features."
- Example 2: Post-call follow-up
  Why it works: Converts discussion into owned next action with date.
  Script: "You confirmed onboarding inconsistency is affecting retention. Next step: workflow mapping session on June 3 with your CS lead; I will send the agenda today."
  Avoid: "Great chat. Let us know if you want to continue."

## Failure Modes
- Storing examples without context of expected outcome.
- Keeping weak examples because they are recent.
- Failing to refresh examples as messaging evolves.
