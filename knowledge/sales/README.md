# Sales Knowledge Pod

## Purpose
Provide execution-ready guidance for Grove sales conversations from first touch through close.

## Scope
Covers discovery, qualification, objection handling, closing, call openers, follow-up emails, and QA artifacts in this folder.

## Inputs
- `_shared` standards: [taxonomy](../_shared/01-taxonomy.md), [quality bar](../_shared/02-quality-bar.md)
- Live prospect conversations
- CRM stage transitions and notes
- Win/loss feedback from sales + leadership

## Decision Rules
- Follow the linked `_shared` taxonomy and quality bar before introducing local conventions.
- Treat these docs as operational playbooks, not narrative brand copy.
- If a rule is updated, add at least one new example and one failure mode.

## Examples
- Use `playbooks/discovery.md` before first meetings to prepare questioning sequence.
- Use `scripts/follow-up-emails.md` immediately after any meeting with a concrete next step.

## Failure Modes
- Reps improvising language that weakens Grove positioning.
- Missing qualification rigor leading to low-intent pipeline.
- Inconsistent objection responses across team members.
