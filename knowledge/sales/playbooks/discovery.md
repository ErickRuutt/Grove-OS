# Discovery Playbook

## Purpose
Run discovery calls that quickly establish fit, urgency, and buyer readiness for Grove.

## Scope
First live conversation with new prospects from inbound or outbound sources.

## Inputs
- ICP definition from [taxonomy](../../_shared/01-taxonomy.md)
- Account background and role context
- Current workflow pain indicators

## Decision Rules
- Open with business-process context before discussing product features.
- Ask for current workflow, failure cost, and owner accountability within first 10 minutes.
- If there is no defined pain owner, treat as weak fit until proven otherwise.
- End with a dated next step or explicitly disqualify.

## Examples
- "Walk me through where your current AI workflow breaks and who feels that pain first."
- "What happens if this is still unresolved in 60 days?"

## Failure Modes
- Letting calls become product demos before problem clarity.
- Accepting vague pain statements without operational examples.
- Ending calls with "we'll follow up" and no committed next step.
