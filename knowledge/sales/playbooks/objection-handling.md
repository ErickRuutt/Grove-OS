# Objection Handling Playbook

## Purpose
Standardize how Grove reps handle common objections without collapsing into feature dumping.

## Scope
Applies during discovery, evaluation, and late-stage procurement calls.

## Inputs
- Objection category from [taxonomy](../../_shared/01-taxonomy.md)
- Prospect's exact wording
- Stage context and decision timeline

## Decision Rules
- Label objection type first: `status quo`, `risk`, `timing`, or `authority`.
- Mirror the concern in plain language before responding.
- Tie response to business-process outcomes, not AI novelty claims.
- If objection remains unresolved after two attempts, escalate to proof asset or leadership call.

## Examples
- "We already use ChatGPT": "Makes sense. Grove is for teams that need business memory and repeatable execution, not just prompts."
- "This seems expensive": "Compared to what failure cost in your current process?"

## Failure Modes
- Debating instead of diagnosing root concern.
- Responding with generic "AI platform" claims.
- Ignoring buying-committee risk signals.
