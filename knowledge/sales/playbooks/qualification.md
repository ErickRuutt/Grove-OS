# Qualification Playbook

## Purpose
Determine whether a prospect should move forward in pipeline with clear, evidence-based fit criteria.

## Scope
Use after discovery and before deep solutioning or technical validation.

## Inputs
- Discovery notes
- Buying process details
- Timeline and ownership signals

## Decision Rules
- Require all four to qualify: explicit pain, accountable owner, decision path, and near-term timeline.
- Mark as `unqualified` if two or more criteria are missing after follow-up.
- Score fit as `strong`, `medium`, or `weak` with one sentence of evidence.

## Examples
- Strong fit: "Ops leader owns decision, pain impacts weekly KPI review, pilot decision in 30 days."
- Weak fit: "Interested in AI generally, no owner, no timeline."

## Failure Modes
- Advancing based on enthusiasm instead of evidence.
- Confusing multi-threaded stakeholder access with true authority.
- Keeping zombie opportunities open.
