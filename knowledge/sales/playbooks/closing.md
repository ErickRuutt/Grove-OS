# Closing Playbook

## Purpose
Convert qualified opportunities into closed-won deals with controlled risk and clear next actions.

## Scope
Late-stage opportunities from proposal through signature.

## Inputs
- Qualification score
- Stakeholder map
- Procurement and legal requirements

## Decision Rules
- Confirm success criteria and implementation owner before commercial terms discussion.
- Present a single recommended path, not multiple loosely scoped options.
- Set a mutual close plan with dates, owners, and blockers.
- If buying committee is unclear, pause close motion and re-qualify.

## Examples
- "To hit your July rollout, we need legal review complete by June 14 and kickoff owner confirmed by June 20."
- "If those dates slip, we should reset go-live expectations now."

## Failure Modes
- Discounting to compensate for weak qualification.
- Closing on verbal enthusiasm without execution ownership.
- Ignoring implementation-readiness risk.
