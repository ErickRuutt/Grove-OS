# Channel Message Matrix v1

## Purpose
Map ICP segment to pain, proof, CTA, and disqualifier so homepage, paid social, and lifecycle email stay aligned.

## Scope
Tier 1 and Tier 2 segments only. Channels covered: website homepage, paid social, email lifecycle.

## Matrix
| Segment | Core Pain | Message Angle | Required Proof | Channel Adaptation | CTA | Disqualifier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 Operator (20-200 ppl, owns cross-team workflows) | Execution drift, broken handoffs, AI usage is inconsistent by person | Grove is the operating system for reliable AI-enabled execution | Before/after workflow reliability metric, named owner, 30-60 day rollout example | Homepage: mechanism + proof early; Paid: pain-led hook with operator language; Email: implementation blueprint sequence | See If Grove Fits Your Team | "If no one owns the workflow, this will not stick." |
| Tier 1 Team Lead (functional owner, process accountability) | Quality variance across team, repeated errors, no shared AI playbook | Turn ad hoc prompting into repeatable team workflows with shared memory | Reduction in rework/escalations, role-based workflow example | Homepage: role outcome block; Paid: team consistency message; Email: role-specific use case | See Workflow Examples | "Not for one-off personal productivity." |
| Tier 2 Leader (clear pain, weak sponsorship) | Knows pain but cannot force cross-functional change | Start with one owned workflow and prove value fast | Pilot-to-activation milestone evidence with decision path | Homepage: lower emphasis; Paid: conservative; Email: nurture with internal buy-in assets | Start Fit Assessment | "Requires an accountable rollout owner before scale." |

## Channel Rules
1. Homepage
- Prioritize strategic clarity and fit filtering over breadth.
- Keep mechanism and proof above the fold plus immediate next section.

2. Paid Social
- Optimize to qualified demo rate, not click-through rate.
- Lead with pain + disqualifier to avoid low-fit volume.
- Avoid generic "AI transformation" language.

3. Email Lifecycle
- Sequence by intent depth:
  - Email 1: problem recognition + fit framing
  - Email 2: mechanism + workflow example
  - Email 3: proof + disqualifier
  - Email 4: direct fit-assessment CTA

## Message QA Checks
- Can the segment see itself in the first sentence?
- Is the proof specific enough to be falsifiable?
- Is there explicit "not for" language?
- Is CTA consistent with intent stage?
