# Golden Examples

## Purpose
Capture high-performing marketing examples that teams can reuse to maintain quality and consistency.

## Scope
Messaging and channel assets aligned with Grove positioning and conversion standards.

## Inputs
- Top-performing campaign snippets
- Homepage and email copy with strong qualification outcomes
- QA rubric references from [rubric](./rubric.md)

## Decision Rules
- Include only examples scoring >= 4 across all rubric categories.
- Each example must include why it works, target segment, and what to avoid.
- Refresh examples when language, objections, or fit criteria shift.

## Examples
- Example 1: Homepage hero line
  Target segment: Operator-led teams with process complexity.
  Why it works: Defines category and outcome in one sentence.
  Copy: "Grove gives your team shared business memory so AI work happens the same way every time."
  Avoid: "The all-in-one AI platform for everyone."
- Example 2: Paid social qualifier ad
  Target segment: Team leads evaluating operational AI rollout.
  Why it works: Filters low fit while inviting serious buyers.
  Copy: "If your AI stack is scattered across tools and people, Grove helps you run it like an operating system."
  Avoid: "See the coolest AI demo of the year."

## Failure Modes
- Saving examples based on style preference instead of outcomes.
- Omitting target-segment context, making examples hard to apply.
- Keeping stale examples after positioning shifts.
