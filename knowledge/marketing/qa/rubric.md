# Marketing QA Rubric

## Purpose
Define how to score marketing assets for positioning clarity, audience fit, and conversion usefulness.

## Scope
Applies to homepage modules, campaign creatives, lifecycle emails, and SEO content in the Marketing pod.

## Inputs
- Asset copy and creative
- Conversion and quality metrics
- [positioning](../messaging/positioning.md)
- [quality bar](../../_shared/02-quality-bar.md)

## Decision Rules
- Score each asset 1-5 across: positioning clarity, ICP fit, proof strength, conversion intent, and cross-channel consistency.
- A pass requires average score >= 4.0 with no category below 3.
- Any score below 3 requires a correction note and owner.

## Examples
- Score 5 ICP fit: copy calls out operator ownership and pre-filters novelty seekers.
- Score 2 proof strength: claims differentiation but provides no evidence or concrete workflow anchor.

## Failure Modes
- Confusing engagement metrics with quality outcomes.
- Inflated scoring to avoid rewriting weak copy.
- QA done in isolation from sales/CS signal.
