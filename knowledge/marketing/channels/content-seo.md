# Content SEO

## Purpose
Capture high-intent organic demand from operators searching for workflow-level AI execution guidance.

## Scope
Topic strategy, content structure standards, and conversion design for SEO-driven assets.

## Inputs
- [icp-segmentation](../messaging/icp-segmentation.md)
- Sales discovery questions and objection language
- Search performance by query cluster
- Product and customer evidence for proof-backed claims

## Decision Rules
- Prioritize problem-led, operator-intent query clusters over broad AI trend terms.
- Tie every content asset to a funnel role and one clear conversion path.
- Build pages around real objections and decision criteria from sales/support.
- Use proof sections that show process depth, not generic AI tips.

## Examples
- High-intent cluster: "how to operationalize AI across teams" / "AI workflow governance".
- Article CTA: "Run a workflow fit assessment" rather than "Try our AI assistant." 
- Content template: pain framing -> decision criteria -> implementation pattern -> Grove fit test.

## Failure Modes
- Publishing top-of-funnel AI commentary with no route to qualified conversion.
- Keyword chasing detached from ICP language.
- Overproducing content before positioning and proof are stable.
