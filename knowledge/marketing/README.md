# Marketing Knowledge Pod

## Purpose
Provide message-market clarity and channel execution guidance so Grove attracts high-intent operators and filters low-fit traffic.

## Scope
Covers positioning, ICP segmentation, value proposition language, paid social, email lifecycle, content SEO, and QA artifacts in this folder.
Includes an operating memo for near-term priorities, instrumentation, and cross-silo asks.

## Inputs
- `_shared` standards: [taxonomy](../_shared/01-taxonomy.md), [quality bar](../_shared/02-quality-bar.md)
- Sales objection patterns and win/loss notes
- Funnel conversion and source-quality signals
- Product proof points and customer outcomes

## Decision Rules
- Prioritize qualified attention over broad reach.
- Every channel tactic must reinforce core positioning: Grove is business-context AI orchestration, not a generic chatbot.
- If a messaging rule changes, add at least one concrete example and one failure mode.

## Examples
- Use `messaging/positioning.md` to keep homepage, ads, and email narrative aligned.
- Use `website/homepage-narrative-spec.md` as the source of truth for homepage sequence and CTA strategy.
- Use `messaging/channel-message-matrix-v1.md` to keep segment-level claims and disqualifiers consistent across channels.
- Use `channels/email-lifecycle.md` to sequence message depth by intent and buying stage.
- Use `metrics/instrumentation-spec.md` for event schema and quality-focused reporting requirements.
- Use `cmo-operating-memo-2026-05-26.md` to execute the current 14-day marketing plan.

## Failure Modes
- Optimizing for clicks while degrading fit quality.
- Writing broad AI copy that could describe any assistant tool.
- Channel teams running disconnected narratives.
